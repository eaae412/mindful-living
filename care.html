Assisted Living

Assisted living at Mindful Care Living means having a comfortable home with caring help close by whenever it’s needed. In our intimate five-bedroom setting, we get to know each resident personally — their routines, preferences, and the things that matter most to them — so support fits naturally into their day.

  • A safe, welcoming residential home
  • Personalized attention in a small setting
  • Support that respects independence and choice
  • Meaningful social connections

24/7 Comprehensive Personal Care

Care is available around the clock, every day. We help with personal care patiently and respectfully, protecting each resident’s privacy and moving at a pace that feels comfortable.

  • Assistance with bathing
  • Help with dressing
  • Mobility support — moving safely around the home
  • Personal hygiene and grooming
  • Help any time of the day or night

Support for Daily Activities

Everyday life is about more than personal care. We help residents stay connected, active, and engaged with the people and places that matter to them.

  • Transportation to medical and personal appointments
  • Help keeping track of appointments
  • Transportation to activities and personal outings
  • Encouragement to take part in activities they enjoy

Medication Management

Taking the right medication at the right time matters for health and peace of mind — for residents and their families. We provide reminders and assistance with the administration of prescribed medications.

  • Reminders when it’s time for medications
  • Assistance with administration of prescribed medications
  • A steady, reliable daily routine

Not sure what kind of support is needed?

Every person is different. Send us a message and we’ll reach out to talk through your situation, answer your questions, and help you decide whether Mindful Care Living is the right fit.
