About Mindful Care Living

About Us

Because every stage of life deserves to be lived with dignity.

Our Story

At Mindful Care Living, we believe care should feel personal, not clinical, and home should be more than just a place to live. It should be a place where you feel safe, where someone knows your name and your story, and where you are treated with kindness, patience, and respect.

Our vision is to create a home where adults who need additional support can experience the comfort of a welcoming environment without losing the independence, individuality, and meaningful connections that make life fulfilling. In our intimate residential setting, we want every resident to feel seen, heard, and valued—not simply as someone receiving care, but as a person with their own story, preferences, and dreams.

Our Mission

Our mission is simple: to make a meaningful difference in the lives of the people we serve, one day at a time.

We are committed to providing compassionate, individualized support that helps residents navigate daily life with dignity, confidence, and a sense of belonging. Whether it’s offering a helping hand with everyday activities, providing transportation to appointments and personal outings, sharing a conversation over a meal, or simply being there when someone needs reassurance, we believe the smallest moments of care can make the biggest difference.

We want our residents to feel that they are not just living in a place that cares for them, but in a home where they truly belong.

Because everyone deserves more than care. Everyone deserves to feel at home.
