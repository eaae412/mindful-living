Life at Mindful Care Living

Amenities & Daily Life

A comfortable home, a helping hand, and days filled with the things you enjoy.

Amenities & support

Everything is designed to make daily life easier, safer, and more enjoyable.

  • Transportation

    Rides to appointments, activities, and personal outings.

  • Appointments

    Support with keeping track of and getting to appointments.

  • Activities

    Support to take part in activities and stay socially connected.

  • Personal care

    Assistance with bathing, dressing, mobility, and hygiene.

  • Medication help

    Reminders and assistance with administration of prescribed medications.

  • Care 24/7

    Help is available around the clock, every day.

Activities

Days at Mindful Care Living are shaped around the people who live here. We encourage residents to keep doing the things they love — and to try something new when they’d like to.

  • Conversation and companionship
  • Games, puzzles, and hobbies
  • Time outdoors when the weather is nice
  • Outings in the community
  • Celebrating special moments together

Dining

Mealtimes are a chance to slow down and share time together. Meals are served right here at home, and we take each resident’s tastes and dietary needs into account.

Sometimes the smallest moments of care — like sharing a conversation over a meal — make the biggest difference.

  • Meals shared together at home
  • Attention to personal tastes and dietary needs
  • Snacks and drinks throughout the day
